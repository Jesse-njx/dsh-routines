dsh-routines — 60-second promo · narration
==========================================
Read aloud while advancing slides. Total ≈ 1 minute (about 8 seconds
per slide). Advance on the slide marker.

[S1 · 0:00–0:08 · title]
Meet dsh-routines: scheduled agents for DeepSeek Harness.
You write a prompt, pick a schedule, and DSH runs it for you — while you sleep.

[S2 · 0:08–0:16 · a routine is one YAML file]
A routine is one tiny YAML file.
A prompt, a cron schedule, a timezone, and where the digest should land.
Human-readable, git-committable — your automation lives right in your repo.

[S3 · 0:16–0:24 · why you want it]
Nightly test triage, morning briefs, dependency checks —
the chores you would run at two a.m., if you were awake.
And when your laptop was asleep? One honest catch-up run on wake.
No backlog replay, ever.

[S4 · 0:24–0:32 · how it works]
Here's the loop: the scheduler watches your routines,
launches each run as its own headless session — full audit log —
and leaves you a digest. Status, duration, session id, everything.

[S5 · 0:32–0:40 · in the terminal]
Manage it all from the terminal.
List what's scheduled, run one right now, pause it, check the logs.

[S6 · 0:40–0:48 · safety]
It runs unattended, so safety comes first:
anything that would prompt gets auto-denied and noted right in the digest.
Hard timeouts, no silent escalation —
a wedged two-a.m. agent can't hold your repo at nine.

[S7 · 0:48–0:56 · get it]
One command and it's yours.
Add dsh-routines to your profile, and from tonight —
run a prompt on a cron, get the digest where you already are.
